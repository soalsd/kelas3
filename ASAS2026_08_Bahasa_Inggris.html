Ringkasan Materi

Numbers, Introduction, & Apologizing

  • Numbers 1–20: one (1) to twenty (20).
  • Greeting & introduction: menyapa ('Hello/Good morning'), menyebutkan nama ('My name is ...') dan umur ('I am ... years old').
  • Apologizing & school supplies: 'Sorry' / 'I am sorry' untuk meminta maaf; kosakata perlengkapan sekolah seperti book, pencil, eraser, ruler, bag.

Hobbies, Likes/Dislikes, & Time

  • Hobbies: reading, drawing, singing, swimming, playing football.
  • Like & dislike: 'I like ...' (suka) dan 'I don't like ...' (tidak suka).
  • Time & daily activities: menanyakan dan menyebutkan waktu ('What time is it? It is seven o'clock') serta aktivitas harian seperti wake up, go to school, study, sleep.

Subjects, Food & Drink, & Requesting

  • School subjects: Math, English, Science, Art, PE.
  • Food & drink: rice, bread, fried chicken, milk, water, juice.
  • Ordering & requesting: menggunakan ungkapan sopan seperti 'Can I have ... please?' dan meresponnya ('Sure, here you are').
