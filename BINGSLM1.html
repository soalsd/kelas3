2. Greeting, Telling Name & Age (Memperkenalkan Diri & Umur)

Saat berkenalan dengan teman baru, gunakan pola ungkapan berikut:

  • Menanyakan nama: "What is your name?" → "My name is Ahmad."
  • Menanyakan umur: "How old are you?" → "I am eight years old." (Saya berumur 8 tahun)
  • Penggunaan To-Be (am/is/are):
    • I am...
    • You / We / They are...
    • He / She / It is...

3. Expression of Apologize (Ungkapan Permintaan Maaf)

Ungkapan ini digunakan ketika kita melakukan kesalahan secara tidak sengaja:

  • Meminta Maaf:
    • "I am sorry for breaking your pencil." (Maafkan saya karena mematahkan pensilmu.)
    • "I am sorry I am late." (Maaf saya terlambat.)
  • Merespons Maaf:
    • "It's okay." (Tidak apa-apa.)
    • "No problem." (Tidak masalah.)
    • "Never mind." (Jangan dipikirkan.)

Latihan Soal SLM 1 (10 PG + 5 Isian)

Bagian A: Pilihan Ganda (10 Soal)

1. What number comes right after "twelve"?

Kunci Jawaban: B. Thirteen

2. Complete the sentence: "We put our pencils and erasers in the ______."

Kunci Jawaban: A. Pencil case

3. A: "How old are you?"
B: "I _____ eight years old."

Kunci Jawaban: B. am

4. You accidentally drop your friend's book. What should you say?

Kunci Jawaban: B. I am so sorry

5. Response to an apology: "I'm sorry for being late." → "______"

Kunci Jawaban: B. It's okay, no problem

6. Sinta loves reading novels and storybooks. Her hobby is...

Kunci Jawaban: A. Reading

7. "Saya tidak suka bermain bola" in English is...

Kunci Jawaban: B. I do not like playing football

8. What is the English translation for number 15?

Kunci Jawaban: B. Fifteen

9. What tool do we use to draw straight lines?

Kunci Jawaban: B. Ruler

10. "Berenang" in English is...

Kunci Jawaban: B. Swimming

Bagian B: Isian Singkat (5 Soal)

1. Translate to English: "Saya berumur sembilan tahun."

Kunci Jawaban: I am nine years old

2. What stationery do you use to clean pencil writing errors?

Kunci Jawaban: Eraser / Rubber

3. Write the English word for number "20".

Kunci Jawaban: Twenty

4. Expression used to say sorry is called expression of ______.

Kunci Jawaban: Apologize / Apology

5. Translate to English: "Saya suka menggambar."

Kunci Jawaban: I like drawing
